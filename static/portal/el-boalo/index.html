Where it is

Forty-five minutes from Madrid, 253 metres above it

North of the city at the foot of the Sierra de Guadarrama. Close enough to use every weekend, far enough to be another world.

The shelf at the foot of the sierra

Not in the mountains and not on the plain. El Boalo sits at 920 m, 253 above Madrid, high enough to hold the climate and low enough to keep the road open. Peak and settlement altitudes are exact; the ground between them is schematic.

Proximity to all that matters

The A-6 or the M-607 from Madrid Centro, the cercanías line at Cercedilla, and Barajas at fifty minutes. Twelve of the seventeen places on this drawing sit inside thirty minutes of the gate: villages, a reservoir, climbing, a summit and a ski lift. Rings are drive times; each place sits on its true bearing.

Cooler summers

Afternoons and especially nights run cooler than the city, the reason madrileños have escaped up here for generations. Two hundred and fifty three metres of altitude, and none of the city’s stored heat after dark.

Indicative. Modelled from the altitude difference and the city’s heat island, not yet verified against AEMET station records.

Four real seasons

Snow on the peaks from November to March, green springs, warm dry summers and a long autumn. Twenty-two degrees separate the January and July highs, which is why the estate is used in every month rather than three.

Hospitality at its best

A wellness hotel and spa on the access contour, turning every home here into a serviced residence. We are in talks with international wellness and destination brands, and with expert Spanish operators.

It is also the showroom. A buyer books a night, swims, eats at the farm table and walks the dehesa before anyone shows them a price. Guests who come for a weekend leave as owners, which is how the hotel sells the homes around it.

A restaurant worth the drive from Madrid

Open fire, long tables, the kitchen garden a few steps away and the farm behind it. What is picked here is cooked here, and what the surrounding land grows arrives on the same table. Regenerative living is not a claim on this estate, it is the menu.

We are in talks with leading figures from the Madrid culinary world to run it, so the restaurant is a destination in its own right rather than a hotel amenity. A restaurant people drive up for brings the estate an audience of its own, and gives every owner somewhere to eat on a Tuesday.

Living with the doors open

Everything here is oriented south and west, at the buildings’ low, quiet scale, so the sierra is in the room rather than framed on a wall. Terraces run straight out into dry-stone walls and dehesa, with no fence to stop the eye.

Half the year is spent outside: long lunches under the pergola, fires lit as the temperature drops, dark skies overhead. Twenty hectares of protected country wrap the estate, so the horizon you buy is the horizon that stays.

Somewhere to work, and somewhere to stop

A study and library on the estate: desks that face the dehesa, quiet corners. Fast connection, no commute, and the door to twenty hectares of open country three steps away.

It is what makes the sierra a place you use on a Wednesday rather than only in August. Work the morning here, walk at lunch, and be back in Madrid in forty-five minutes when the week demands it.

The club

Thermal water and cold plunge in the rock, a sauna facing the peaks, treatment rooms, a gym and studio, and courts and pools beyond. Everything a serious day in the sierra asks for afterwards: come down off La Pedriza, in from the gravel trails, back from Navacerrada, and the recovery is a two-minute walk from your door.

Space for the active hours and the still ones. Train early, swim at noon, and read by the fire while the light goes off the granite. Owners hold membership as part of the estate, and the hotel runs it to a standard that has to satisfy paying guests as well, which is what keeps it sharp year after year.

The programme

Built on what the area is already known for

Every part puts guests and owners straight into the landscape, and gives the hotel another reason to be visited.

Wellness hotel & spa

A boutique hotel and spa on the access contour, serving owners and guests, and making every home a serviced residence.

Farm & table

A working farm and kitchen garden on the east meadow, developed with local farmers, feeding a restaurant meant to be a destination in itself.

Courts & play

Padel and tennis, pools, a fitness pavilion, and timber play structures for children.

Climbing & mountain

Guided climbing and bouldering on La Pedriza, nearly a thousand routes minutes away. Walking and trail running from the gate, and La Maliciosa at 2,227 m above the village.

Snow

Navacerrada and Valdesquí about thirty minutes further up the sierra, and the peaks under snow from the estate all winter.

Water

Embalse de Santillana, ten minutes away: open-water swimming, paddle and kayak, birdwatching.

The village

Mataelpino, the next village in the same municipality, runs its boloencierro each summer. Local producers at the table, festival weekends.

Four things read straight off the table

  • No peer combines what we have

    A metropolitan market of about seven million, larger than Lisbon or Athens, forty-five minutes away, and a genuine mountain landscape. The city-adjacent peers are urban seafront. The great rural estates sit two to three hours out.

  • Four seasons, so demand runs all year

    At 920 m the estate is a cool-air escape from Madrid’s summers, has snow within reach in winter, and temperate spring and autumn. Mediterranean peers are summer-led, Alpine peers winter-led.

  • The brand premium is there to take

    Branded residences in resort locations command a 39% premium over unbranded stock, the highest of any setting, against a 33% global average.

  • We ask less than our own city

    Every peer charges a premium over the city that feeds it. At 0.7 times the Madrid metre, we are the only one asking less.

  • This category trades at double elsewhere

    The median retreat in section 06 sells at 2.2 times the average metre in the city that feeds it. We ask 0.7 times Madrid. Reaching the bottom of the category is not a forecast, it is a correction.

The demand

The buyer already lives in Madrid

Some 65,000 declarations in the region clear €150,000 a year, the wealth sits in the corridor facing the sierra, and two thirds of those households have yet to buy a second home.

Why now

  • 64,640 declarations

    Madrid tax declarations above €150,000 a year, of which 6,573 above €601,000. Madrid holds 44% of Spain’s top earners.

  • 2 in 3 have no second home

    Households over €5,000 a month who do not yet own one, and those that do hold it on the coast, hours away. The sierra has no serviced stock.

  • +82% population growth

    El Boalo over twenty years, driven by Madrid families moving to the sierra. The ten corridor towns close ~2,100 dwelling sales a year.

  • Madrid prime is compounding

    Five to six per cent a year, three years running, with Knight Frank ranking Madrid at the top of Europe’s prime markets. New build hit a record €5,172/m².

  • The corridor has nothing to sell

    A handful of townhouse and semi-detached schemes in the pipeline, none of them serviced. The estate arrives into a market repricing upward with nothing like it to buy.

The upside

Land is fixed, Madrid is growing

The category already trades at double elsewhere. Everything not yet done here is what the early buyer is paid for.

Where the value goes

The launch buyer takes unbranded, unbuilt land risk. The 2030 buyer takes a finished, serviced home on a trading estate with a brand on it and nothing left to sell. The ladder is our pricing policy at each milestone, not a market forecast.

The ladder is BYLD’s own pricing policy at each milestone, not a forecast of the market. Indicative and confirmed at Plan Parcial. Nothing here is an offer, a contract or a guarantee of value.

Land is the part you cannot make more of

A hotel can be built anywhere. Seven hectares at 920 m, forty-five minutes from a capital and backed onto a national park, cannot be replaced.

Madrid has no rural product

Visitors come to Madrid for the city and leave having seen only the city. The sierra is forty-five minutes away with mountains, water, rock, snow and a working countryside, and nothing serviced to stay in. The demand exists; the product does not.

The estate can grow

Adjacent parcels can be brought into the estate, farmed and grazed regeneratively, so nothing is ever built between these homes and the sierra. Reclassification is a possibility, not a plan and not an entitlement.

Regenerative living needs projects that prove it

Every operator and every buyer now claims the language. Very few can show the land, the farm and the trees behind it. An estate that farms its own surroundings and keeps three quarters of itself open is demonstrable, and demonstrable is what the market is short of.
